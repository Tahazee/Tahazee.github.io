Overview

iGlasses is a smart wearable system designed to give visually impaired users real-time perception of their surroundings — entirely on-device, no cloud dependency.

I architected the complete AI stack: multi-class obstacle detection CNNs in PyTorch, optimized via TensorRT INT8 quantization for the Jetson Orin Nano. Dual IMX219-83 stereo cameras provide depth data for proximity estimation. A lightweight NLP engine handles voice commands offline, with a priority-based speech dispatcher ensuring emergency alerts always preempt routine descriptions.

Engineering Challenges
Achieving <400ms end-to-end latency required layer-by-layer TensorRT profiling and INT8 calibration on domain-representative data — not just default calibration.
The NLP speech dispatcher needed strict priority logic so emergency alerts (imminent collision) always preempted routine descriptions (general obstacle).
Featureless environments (uniform floor surfaces) caused false-negative detection — solved with augmented training data emphasizing edge-case textures.
Key Learnings
Quantization-aware training and TensorRT profiling are essential for real-world edge deployment — every layer must earn its compute budget.
Hardware constraints force architectural discipline. The best model is not the most accurate — it is the one that is accurate enough and fast enough.
Offline NLP is tractable at edge scale when scoped correctly. Limiting the vocabulary to domain-specific commands dramatically reduces model size and latency.
