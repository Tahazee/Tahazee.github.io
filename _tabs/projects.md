## 🔴 1. Ulurover – Mars Rover Vision Localization & Sensor Fusion
**Role**: Team Leader | Autonomous System Engineer  
**Organization**: Ulurover (Mars Rover Team), Bursa, Turkey  
**Timeline**: Sept 2023 – Jan 2025  

> Engineered real-time spatial feature extraction and state estimation pipelines for an autonomous Mars Rover competing in international robotics challenges.

### Key Highlights & Technical Accomplishments:
- **Spatial Feature Extraction**: Implemented **ORB-SLAM3** coupled with **ZED2 stereo vision** for real-time 3D feature tracking and robust visual odometry in unstructured terrain.
- **Sensor Fusion**: Designed an **Extended Kalman Filter (EKF)** node integrating high-frequency IMU telemetry with visual state estimation, significantly reducing drift.
- **Team & Project Leadership**: Directed a multidisciplinary engineering team of **25+ members** across robotics sub-systems, securing **250k TL** in corporate sponsorships to make it the highest-funded technical team at the university.

**Tech Stack**: `ROS2` `Python` `C++` `ORB-SLAM3` `ZED2 Stereo Camera` `EKF` `IMU` `Pixhawk` `Linux`
